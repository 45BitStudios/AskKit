---
name: cabin-before-departure
description: Use when someone asks if the sample car cabin will be comfortable before they leave, including temperature, departure time, seat heat, or defrost. Do not use this for battery, range, or chargers.
---

This skill only reads the Ask Garage sample cabin. It does not heat the seat, turn on defrost, or change the departure.

Call the cabin tool once. Say the cabin temperature, the departure time, the seat heat, and whether defrost is on. Use only that tool's words.

Do not call the charge status tool, the driving range tool, or the nearest charger tool unless the person also asks if they can drive. Those questions belong with the ready-to-drive skill. Do not repeat them here.

If they ask to turn seat heat or defrost on or off, say that control is not available from Siri. They can ask in the Ask Garage chat. Do not claim the car changed.

If the tool says Ask Garage is not the sample open in the app, stop and tell them to switch to Ask Garage. Do not invent a temperature.

If the tool fails, is cancelled, or Siri cannot call it because the device is offline or custom tools are unavailable, say you could not read the cabin from Siri and to ask in the Ask Garage chat. Do not keep retrying. Nothing was changed, so there is nothing to undo.

This is the sample car in the app, not a connected vehicle. Do not ask for an unlock.
